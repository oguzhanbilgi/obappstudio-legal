Privacy Policy & Terms of Use

Last updated: October 23, 2026 • Effective Date: October 23, 2026

1. Overview & Offline-First Architecture

VeloHours: Work Hours & Pay (referred to as "we", "us", or "the App") is developed by ObAppStudio. We believe that your work hours, hourly rates, clients, and financial earnings are completely private. VeloHours is built with an offline-first architecture: all shifts, timer logs, and worker profiles are stored exclusively on your local device (via SQLite) and are never transmitted to our servers.

2. Data Collection and Usage

We do not require you to create an account, log in, or provide personal credentials. Information you enter (such as worker name, hourly wages, client names, and notes) remains on your phone. When you generate an official PDF timesheet, the document is rendered locally on your device and shared directly via your chosen sharing method (such as WhatsApp, Email, or Print).

3. In-App Purchases & Subscriptions

VeloHours offers optional Pro upgrades (such as Lifetime Pro or Annual access) to unlock unlimited timesheet exports and remove ads. All payments are processed securely by Google Play Billing and validated via RevenueCat. We do not have access to your credit card, debit card, or banking details.

4. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The free version of VeloHours displays non-intrusive mobile banner and rewarded ads via Google AdMob. AdMob may use anonymized advertising identifiers (such as Google Advertising ID) in compliance with Google Play Policies. You can upgrade to Pro at any time to permanently remove all advertisements.
