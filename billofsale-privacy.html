🇺🇸 Privacy Policy (English)

Summary: Bill of Sale: Auto & As-Is is built with an offline-first privacy architecture. All vehicle contracts, buyer/seller personal identities, VINs, addresses, and digital signatures remain strictly on your local device storage.

1. Information We Do Not Collect

We do not operate remote servers to store or monitor private contracts created in the app. Your full legal names, physical addresses, driver's license numbers, vehicle identification numbers (VINs), transaction sale prices, and digital signature records are stored exclusively in your local device SQLite database.

2. Third-Party Services & Advertising ID

The application integrates compliant third-party software development kits (SDKs):

  • Google Mobile Ads (AdMob): For non-subscribers, AdMob may access standard advertising identifiers (Google Advertising ID / AAID) to deliver relevant advertisements and fraud prevention in accordance with Google Play Developer Policies.
  • Google Play Billing & RevenueCat: Subscription purchases and lifetime passes are handled directly and securely through Google Play In-App Billing. We never receive or store your credit card or financial payment details.

3. Permissions Used

  • INTERNET: Required for Google AdMob banner display and Google Play subscription verification.
  • ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE: Used to determine online/offline status gracefully.
  • POST_NOTIFICATIONS: Optional permission to notify you about saved deals or export status.
  • AD_ID: Declared for Google Advertising ID on Android 13+ devices.

4. Data Retention and Deletion

Because all legal documents are kept locally, you have total control over your data. Deleting a transaction or uninstalling the app permanently purges all saved contracts, buyer/seller names, and digital signatures from your device.

5. Contact Us

For any privacy inquiries or legal support, contact: oguzhanbilgidev@gmail.com

🇹🇷 Gizlilik Politikası (Türkçe)

Özet: Bill of Sale uygulaması tamamen yerel (offline-first) mimariyle geliştirilmiştir. Sözleşmeleriniz, alıcı/satıcı kimlik bilgileri ve dijital imzalarınız sadece kendi telefonunuzda saklanır.
