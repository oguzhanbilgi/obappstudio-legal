Privacy Policy

In short

  • No account is needed. Your game progress is saved only on your device.
  • We do not run servers, we do not use analytics or crash-reporting tools, and we do not sell personal data.
  • The game shows Google AdMob ads. Google may collect device and usage data for ads, as explained in section 4.
  • The game asks for your date of birth once. The date itself is not stored; only your age group is kept on your device.
  • If optional in-app purchases are offered, Google Play processes the payment. We never see your card details.

1. Who we are

Squishy Merge (“the game”) is an Android game published by ObAppStudio (“we”, “us”). This policy explains what information the game handles, where it is stored and who receives it. It applies to the game with the package name com.obappstudio.squishymerge.

2. Information stored only on your device

The game saves the following in its private storage on your device. It is used only to run the game and is never sent to us.

CategoryWhat it includes
Game progressUnlocked levels and stars, scores, in-game currency (Dough), collection pieces, power-ups, missions, daily rewards, achievements, titles and player level
SettingsLanguage, sound and vibration preferences, and whether the tutorial is finished
Age groupYour age group (13–17 or 18 and over) and, for 13–17, the date you turn 18. Your date of birth itself is not stored and never leaves your device.
Purchase recordsIf you buy something: the product, the time, the delivery status and a one-way fingerprint (hash) of the Google Play purchase token, so the same purchase is never delivered twice

The game does not ask for your name, email address, phone number, location, contacts, photos or files. Your player name is a fixed name, and your avatar is a collection piece from the game. Android cloud backup is turned off for the game’s data.

3. Your age and ads

Before ads are shown, the game asks for your date of birth on a neutral screen. The game uses it only to work out your age group and then discards it. For players aged 13–17, ad requests are marked for teen treatment (no personalised ads) and limited to teen-appropriate ad content. You can update your age in Settings → Age Info.

4. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The game shows banner, interstitial (full-screen) and rewarded ads from Google AdMob. Rewarded ads play only when you choose to watch one. To show and measure ads, and for analytics and fraud prevention, Google may collect:

  • your device’s advertising ID and app set ID;
  • your IP address and an approximate location derived from it;
  • device information, such as model, operating system and language;
  • app interaction information, such as ads viewed and tapped.

Depending on your consent and settings, ads may be personalised or non-personalised. If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, you are asked for your choice through Google’s consent form (User Messaging Platform) and can change it at any time in the game under Settings → “Privacy Options”. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your Android device settings.

Learn more: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

5. In-app purchases (Google Play Billing)

The game may offer optional in-app purchases, such as power-up packs, Dough packs, a Starter Pack and Remove Ads. Payments are processed by Google Play under Google’s terms and privacy policy; we never see your card number or other payment details. The game talks only to Google Play’s billing service to show prices, start a purchase and confirm delivery. Remove Ads turns off banner ads and automatic full-screen ads; rewarded ads you choose to watch stay available.

6. What we do not do

  • We do not require or offer user accounts.
  • We do not run servers that receive your game data.
  • We do not use analytics or crash-reporting tools.
  • We do not sell or rent personal data.

7. Keeping and deleting your data

Your game data stays on your device. Uninstalling the game deletes it from the device. Purchases themselves are recorded by Google Play with your Google account. Data processed by Google is kept according to Google’s own policies.

8. Security

Your game data is kept in the game’s private storage, which other apps cannot normally read. Connections made by the Google services above are encrypted.

9. Your choices and rights

Because we do not hold your data, there is nothing for us to access, correct or delete on a server. Depending on where you live — for example under the EU or UK GDPR or Turkey’s Personal Data Protection Law No. 6698 (KVKK) — you may have rights to access, correct or delete personal data, or to object to its processing. For data processed by Google, use the controls described above or contact Google. You can also write to us and we will help where we can.

10. Children

The game is not directed at children under 13. The age screen accepts only dates of birth for players aged 13 and over, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the game changes. The new version will be published on this page with a new effective date.

Reklamlar gösterilmeden önce oyun, doğum tarihini nötr bir ekranda sorar. Yaşını Ayarlar → Yaş bilgisi bölümünden güncelleyebilirsin.

4. Reklamlar (Google AdMob)

Oyun Google AdMob’dan banner, geçiş (tam ekran) ve ödüllü reklamlar gösterir. Ödüllü reklamlar yalnızca sen izlemeyi seçtiğinde oynar. Reklamları göstermek ve ölçmek, analiz yapmak ve sahtekârlığı önlemek için Google şunları toplayabilir:

  • cihazının reklam kimliği ve uygulama grubu kimliği (app set ID);
  • IP adresin ve bundan çıkarılan yaklaşık konum;
  • model, işletim sistemi ve dil gibi cihaz bilgileri;
  • görüntülenen ve dokunulan reklamlar gibi uygulama etkileşim bilgileri.

Onayına ve ayarlarına bağlı olarak reklamlar kişiselleştirilmiş veya kişiselleştirilmemiş olabilir. Avrupa Ekonomik Alanı, Birleşik Krallık veya İsviçre’deysen tercihin Google’ın onay formu (User Messaging Platform) ile sorulur; bu tercihi istediğin zaman oyunda Ayarlar → “Gizlilik seçenekleri” bölümünden değiştirebilirsin. Reklam kimliğini Android cihaz ayarlarından sıfırlayabilir veya silebilirsin.

Ayrıntılar: Google’ın, hizmetlerini kullanan site ve uygulamalardan gelen bilgileri nasıl kullandığı.

5. Uygulama içi satın almalar (Google Play Faturalandırma)

Oyun güç paketleri, Hamur paketleri, Başlangıç Paketi ve Reklamları Kaldır gibi isteğe bağlı uygulama içi satın almalar sunabilir. Ödemeler Google’ın koşulları ve gizlilik politikası kapsamında Google Play tarafından işlenir; kart numaranı veya diğer ödeme bilgilerini hiçbir zaman görmeyiz. Oyun yalnızca fiyatları göstermek, satın almayı başlatmak ve teslimi onaylamak için Google Play’in faturalandırma hizmetiyle konuşur. Reklamları Kaldır, banner reklamları ve otomatik tam ekran reklamları kapatır; kendi isteğinle izlediğin ödüllü reklamlar kullanılabilir kalır.
