# obappstudio-legal

Public privacy policies and terms of use for ObAppStudio's Android apps, published with GitHub Pages at
https://oguzhanbilgi.github.io/obappstudio-legal/

| App | Package | App page | Privacy policy | Terms of use |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| VeloHours | `com.mobilefactory.velohours` | `velohours.html` | `velohours-privacy.html` | `velohours-terms.html` |
| RentLedger Pro | `com.mobilefactory.rentledger` | `rentledger.html` | `rentledger-privacy.html` | `rentledger-terms.html` |
| VeloQuote | `com.mobilefactory.veloquote` | `veloquote.html` | `veloquote-privacy.html` | `veloquote-terms.html` |
| Bill of Sale: Auto & As-Is | `com.mobilefactory.billofsale` | `billofsale.html` | `billofsale-privacy.html` | `billofsale-terms.html` |

Other files:

- `index.html`: app showcase
- `privacy.html`: studio-level privacy policy covering all apps, with links to each app's own policy
- `assets/site.css`: shared styles
- `app-ads.txt`: AdMob seller verification (do not edit)

All policy and terms pages are bilingual (English first, then Turkish, anchors `#en` and `#tr`).
Use the `<app>-privacy.html` and `<app>-terms.html` URLs in Google Play Console and in each app.
